Design Rationale

The Mark: An open arc with an anchor dot. The arc is almost-closed — deliberately not. It signals a thought mid-release, a head clearing, a loop that doesn't trap you. The inner arc (ghost ring) adds depth at large sizes without adding noise at small ones. The dot grounds it — one point of focus, which is exactly what the app delivers.

Why not the medal: The ribbon/trophy mark signals achievement and performance pressure — the exact shame spiral Mind Clear is designed to interrupt. This mark signals release, clarity, and calm instead.

Gold accent: Used only on the dot in dark contexts. Restraint here is the point — one warm moment in a minimal mark.
