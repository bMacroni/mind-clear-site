## Full Description

You've tried the planners. Downloaded the apps. Set up the systems.

And then life happened. Or your brain happened. And none of it stuck.

Mind Clear starts where you actually are: head full of things, not sure where to begin, feeling like everyone else has figured something out that you haven't.

You haven't missed it. The other apps just weren't built for you.

---

**BRAIN DUMP. FINALLY USEFUL.**

Open the app. Type everything in your head: tasks, worries, half-formed ideas, things you forgot to do last week. All of it.

The AI sorts it out. It breaks your thoughts into goals, one-time tasks, recurring tasks, and routines. No setup. No templates. No "first, create a project folder."

Just your thoughts, turned into a plan.

---

**GOALS THAT DON'T COLLECT DUST**

Most planning apps make you build the plan yourself. Mind Clear builds it for you.

Tell it a goal ("run a 5K," "get on top of my bills," "stop feeling behind at work") and it creates a step-by-step plan you can actually start on. When you're stuck on a task, the app breaks it down smaller. When something feels impossible to start, it helps you find a different angle.

The AI doesn't just organize. It gets you unstuck.

---

**ROUTINES THAT STICK**

Habits are hard when your brain craves novelty. Mind Clear builds routines around your real life, morning, evening, or whenever, and keeps them visible so they don't disappear into a forgotten menu.

Miss a day? The app moves on. No shame. No broken streaks. Just tomorrow.

---

**ONE TASK. RIGHT NOW.**

When everything feels urgent, nothing gets done. Focus mode shows you one task at a time, just the next thing. Not the whole list. Not a wall of obligations.

One win. Then another.

---

**FREE TRIAL + PRICING**

Try everything free for 21 days. No credit card required.

After that: the free tier includes tasks, your daily view, and basic routines, enough to get started and see how it fits your brain. Premium ($4.99/month) unlocks unlimited goals, unlimited routines, and the full AI feature set: Brain Dump, AI Goal Planning, and the Stuck Task Helper.

---

**BUILT BY SOMEONE WHO GETS IT**

Mind Clear was built by an Army veteran and father of two who spent years not knowing why his brain worked the way it did.

When he finally connected the dots on his ADHD as an adult, a lot of things suddenly made sense, including years of struggling with apps that assumed he could already organize his thoughts. After getting sober in 2025, the ADHD came back full force. No more workarounds. He built the app he needed.

That's not a marketing story. That's just what happened.
