Two Concepts

Both marks are built around the same feeling: forward momentum, things not piling up, space opening ahead. Two different ways to get there.

Concept A
The Arrow That Isn't
Two angled strokes that converge forward — reads as motion without being a literal arrow. The gap between the strokes is the point: space opening up ahead of you.

The read: Two strokes converging to a single forward point. No arrowhead — the motion is implied by the geometry, not declared by a symbol. The faint vertical tail on the left side is the "before" — where you came from, slightly visible but not the story. The point is where you're going.
Concept B
The Unblocked
A container with a clean opening on the forward edge. Structure on three sides — the frame that holds things together. Open on the right: the way forward is clear. Relief built into the shape.
