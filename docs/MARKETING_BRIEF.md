# Mind Clear — Marketing Brief

**Version:** 1.0  
**Date:** 2026-04-15  
**Audience:** Marketing agent, copywriters, social media team, app store managers

---

## 1. What Is Mind Clear?

Mind Clear is an AI-powered goal and task management app built specifically for people who feel overwhelmed — particularly those managing ADHD, anxiety, or depression. It combines a structured productivity system with a minimalist design and an AI layer that does the heavy lifting: turning a brain full of scattered thoughts into a clear, prioritized plan.

The core promise: **less mental noise, more forward motion.**

Mind Clear is available on **Android** at launch. **iOS support is planned for a future release.**

---

## 2. Target Audience

### Primary
- Adults with ADHD who struggle to start, prioritize, or finish tasks
- People managing anxiety or depression whose cognitive load makes standard productivity tools feel overwhelming or shame-inducing

### Secondary
- Anyone who gets stuck in planning paralysis, perfectionism, or task avoidance
- People who've tried apps like Todoist, Notion, or Things and found them too complex, too gamified, or not built for their brain

### What they have in common
- They want to feel clearer, not more organized
- They need momentum-building tools, not productivity systems
- Standard "streaks-or-you-fail" habit apps trigger shame; they need something gentler
- They often know what needs doing but feel paralyzed actually starting

## 4. Core Features

### 4.1 Brain Dump
The flagship feature. Users type everything on their mind — tasks, worries, ideas, obligations — without structure or format. The AI then:
- Classifies each item by type (goal, task, routine, event)
- Removes duplicates and noise
- Prioritizes the list
- Converts it into structured goals and tasks in the app

**The experience:** Most users feel meaningfully clearer within minutes of their first Brain Dump.

**Who it's for:** Anyone who starts the day with a swirling head and no clear place to begin.

**Availability:** Premium feature. Shown to free users with an upgrade prompt.

---

### 4.2 AI Goal Planning
Users enter a goal — any goal — and the AI generates a full plan. Each goal breaks down into:
- **One-time tasks** — discrete actions to complete the goal
- **Recurring tasks** — repeated actions that need to happen on a schedule
- **Routines** — habits to build that support the goal

The user can choose a complexity level (Simple, Moderate, or Detailed), preview the plan before saving, and reconfigure it if it doesn't fit.

**Availability:** Premium feature.

---

### 4.3 Tasks and Focus Mode
A full task management system with:
- **Due dates**, **priorities** (Low / Medium / High, shown with gold shading), and **recurrence** (daily, weekly, custom patterns)
- **Today's Focus**: The user pins one task as their active focus. Everything else fades away.
- **Celebratory completion**: When a focused task is completed, the card slides away with animated positive feedback ("Crushing it!", "On fire!") and haptic response
- **Auto-scheduling**: The app can automatically schedule tasks into open calendar slots based on user preferences
- **Overdue drip**: Overdue tasks are gradually rescheduled onto upcoming days automatically — no shameful backlog pile

---

### 4.4 Stuck Task Helper
When a user marks their focused task as something they can't start, the AI offers two modes:

- **Break it down**: Generates 3-5 concrete micro-steps so small the task becomes startable
- **Make it interesting**: Offers 2-3 reframes that make the task feel less daunting or more meaningful

The user can also flag "I can't do this today" to remove it from focus without guilt.

**Availability:** Premium feature (free users see a locked state with paywall prompt).

---

### 4.5 Routines and Streak System
A habit-building system designed to be forgiving:
- **Daily, weekly, or monthly** frequency with support for multiple completions per period (e.g., "3x per week")
- **Streak tracking** with visual status: Active (fire), New (seedling), Broken (heart)
- **Backdating**: Forgot to log? Log it for yesterday
- **Completion heatmap**: Monthly calendar view showing habit history on each routine card
- **Undo last completion**: Fix accidental taps

---

### 4.6 Calendar Integration
- Full **Google Calendar sync** (two-way)
- Tasks with due dates appear in the calendar view
- Calendar events imported into the app
- Today's schedule surfaced on the home screen widget (Android)

---

### 4.7 Android Home Screen Widget
A 4×4 home screen widget showing:
- Today's tasks
- Recurring tasks
- Routines for the day
- Calendar events
- Dark/light theme toggle
- Strikethrough for completed items

- Limited number of goals and routines (basic planning available)

### Premium — $4.99/month
- Unlimited goals and routines
- AI Brain Dump

- Stuck Task Helper (Break it down / Make it interesting)

### Trial
- **21-day free trial** — full Premium access, no payment required upfront
- After trial: prompted to subscribe or drop to free tier

## 6. Visual Identity

### Design Philosophy
Intentionally minimal. The interface is black and white with near-zero decoration. This is a deliberate choice for the ADHD/anxiety audience — visual noise is a distraction and cognitive cost. The app should feel calm and focused, not exciting or gamified.

### Color System
| Token | Value | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Primary | `#000000` | Buttons, active states, icons |
| Background | `#FFFFFF` / `#F9F9F9` | App surfaces |
| Text Primary | `#111111` | All primary content |
| Text Secondary | `#444444` | Captions, metadata |
| Gold Accent | `#D4AF37` | Priority indicators, premium feature markers |
| Success | `#10B981` | Completions, confirmations |
| Error | `#EF4444` | Errors, destructive actions |

### Tone in UI Copy
- Direct and warm. No corporate language.
- Encourages without pressuring. ("No pressure — just explore.")
- Acknowledges that hard days happen. ("Life happens. You can log for yesterday.")
- Short sentences. No unnecessary words.

## 9. Technical Highlights (for press/media context)

- **Offline-first**: The app works without internet. Changes sync automatically when connection is restored.
- **AI engine**: Google Gemini 2.5 Flash (primary), Groq llama-3.1 (fast mode)
- **Privacy**: All user data is scoped by row-level security — users can only access their own data
- **No data sharing**: Google OAuth tokens are encrypted at rest with AES-256-GCM

## 11. What to Avoid in Messaging

- Do **not** position Mind Clear as a general productivity tool. It is not Notion, Todoist, or Asana for everyone.
- Do **not** emphasize streaks as a core hook — the audience has often been burned by streak-loss shame in other apps. Lead with forgiveness and flexibility.
- Do **not** use the names MindGarden or Foci — these are deprecated names.
- Do **not** over-promise the AI. It augments the user's thinking; it doesn't replace their judgment.
- Avoid corporate wellness language ("optimize your workflow", "maximize output", "productivity hacks").
- Avoid pressure framing. The audience is sensitive to shame and perfectionism triggers.
