# Mind Clear Marketing Site — Project Instructions

## What This Project Is

This is the marketing website for **Mind Clear**, an AI-powered goal and task management app built for people with ADHD, anxiety, and depression. The site runs on Next.js 14 + React 18 + Tailwind CSS, deployed on Vercel.

The goal: **promote the Mind Clear app and increase our conversion rate.**

Mind Clear is Android-only at launch. iOS is planned but not yet available.

---

## The Product in One Sentence

Mind Clear turns mental overwhelm into a clear, prioritized plan — using AI that acts (creates tasks, plans goals, breaks down blockers), not AI that just gives advice.

---

## Marketing Rules — Follow These Exactly

### Brand Voice

Read `docs/brand-voice.md` before writing ANY copy. The short version:

- **Tone:** Direct, warm, human. Like a calm friend who gets it — not a corporate wellness brand.
- **Perspective:** We speak to people who feel overwhelmed, not people who want to be more productive.
- **Sentence structure:** Short. Clear. No unnecessary words.
- **Emotional register:** Gentle encouragement, never pressure. Acknowledge hard days. Never shame.

### What to Say

- Lead with the feeling: "Clear your head" > "Manage your tasks"
- Brain Dump is the hero feature — it turns a swirling head into a plan in minutes
- The AI does things (creates tasks, builds plans, breaks down blockers) — it doesn't just chat
- The streak system forgives. Missing a day isn't failure.
- Focus mode shows one task at a time. One win at a time.
- Minimalist black-and-white UI is intentional — less visual noise for overwhelmed brains

### What NEVER to Say

- **Never use these names:** MindGarden, Foci (deprecated — do not reference anywhere)
- **Never position as a general productivity tool.** This is not Notion, Todoist, or Asana for everyone.
- **Never lead with streaks** as a selling point. The audience has been burned by streak-shame in other apps.
- **Never use corporate wellness language:** "optimize your workflow," "maximize output," "productivity hacks," "in today's digital landscape," "unlock your potential"
- **Never use pressure framing.** No urgency tactics, no shame triggers, no "you're falling behind"
- **Never over-promise the AI.** It augments thinking; it doesn't replace judgment.
- **Never use AI-sounding phrases:** "delve into," "it's important to note," "in the realm of," "game-changer," "revolutionize"

### Approved Messaging Hierarchy

**Primary headline:**
> "Clear your head. One step at a time."

**Supporting lines (use as needed):**
> "Dump everything on your mind. Mind Clear turns it into a plan."
> "Built for ADHD brains. Gentle on bad days."
> "The only productivity app that helps you when you're stuck."
> "One task. Right now. That's all."

**Tagline options:**
- "Think less. Do more."
- "For brains that work differently."
- "Finally, a planner that gets it."

## Target Audience

**Primary:** Adults with ADHD who struggle to start, prioritize, or finish tasks. People managing anxiety or depression where cognitive load makes standard productivity tools overwhelming or shame-inducing.

**Secondary:** Anyone stuck in planning paralysis, perfectionism, or task avoidance. People who tried Todoist/Notion/Things and found them too complex or not built for their brain.

**What they have in common:** They want to feel clearer, not more organized. They need momentum, not systems. Streak-or-fail apps trigger shame. They often know what to do but feel paralyzed starting.

---

## Competitive Positioning

Mind Clear is NOT competing on features with Todoist or Notion. The position is:

**"The only end-to-end planning app that starts from mental overwhelm and walks users to a clear, actionable day — with AI that helps when things get hard."**

Key differentiators to emphasize:
1. Brain Dump (scattered thoughts → structured plan in minutes)
2. AI that acts, not advises (creates tasks, plans goals, generates micro-steps)
3. Gentle by design (forgiving streaks, no shame, calm UI)
4. Stuck Task Helper (when you can't start, it breaks it down or reframes it)
5. Minimalist UI (intentionally low-stimulation for ADHD/anxiety brains)
