Design rationale

The mark: a waveform resolving to calm. The left side of the mark reads as a classic EEG/neural trace — irregular, high-amplitude, scattered. Moving right, it resolves into a clean flat line. Read left to right, the mark tells the whole story: from chaos to clarity. The amber segment marks the exact moment of resolution — the transition point where overwhelm becomes focus. That's what the app does and what the research supports.

Typography: IBM Plex. IBM Plex Sans + Mono is the typeface of scientific publishing and developer tooling — rigorous without being cold. "LAB" is set in spaced caps in the mono variant, giving it the feel of a proper scientific designation (like a research unit) rather than a startup tagline.

Color: navy + amber. Deep navy pulls from academic and scientific publishing — journals, textbook covers, university logos. The amber is warm enough to prevent clinical sterility, and it bridges to the gold accent from the original Mind Clear identity, keeping a visual thread between the two brands.

Background grid. The subtle grid pattern on this page (and recommended for Lab-branded materials) evokes lab paper, graph paper, scientific notation. It adds context without decoration.
